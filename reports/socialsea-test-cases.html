Authentication and Session Management

Covers register, login, token refresh, and rejection paths for unauthenticated access.

Case Scenario Steps Expected Result
AUTH-01Register a new account with valid email, password, and profile basics.Open /register; submit valid details; continue through first-time setup or landing flow.Account is created and the user is authenticated with the correct landing route.
AUTH-02Login with valid credentials.Open /login; enter a known email and password; submit the form.User is authenticated, session cookies are set, and protected pages become available.
AUTH-03Login with invalid credentials is rejected.Open /login; enter the wrong password or an unknown email; submit the form.An error is shown and no auth cookies or session state are created.
AUTH-04Refresh token rotation succeeds and invalid refresh tokens are cleared.Call the refresh endpoint with a valid refresh cookie; repeat with an expired or invalid token.Valid refresh returns a new access token cookie, while invalid refresh clears cookies and returns unauthorized.

Feed Pages, Posts, and Saved Content

Validates the main feed page, search and filter controls, post viewer actions, long-video mode, and saved-state flows.

Case Scenario Steps Expected Result
FEED-01Open the main feed as an authenticated user.Sign in, navigate to /feed, wait for loading to finish, and scroll the visible timeline.The protected feed route loads posts and media without redirecting the authenticated user away.
FEED-02Search the feed by caption or username and then clear the search.Type a known caption or username in the search box, confirm the list narrows, and then clear the field.Only matching posts remain while the search is active, and clearing the search restores the full feed.
FEED-03Switch between Feed and Video modes.Click the Video tab to open the long-form layout, then switch back to the Feed tab.Video mode shows long-form cards and Feed mode returns to the standard timeline layout.
FEED-04Apply feed category filters and sub-filters.Open the feed options, choose a category such as study or gaming, and pick one of the sub-filters when available.The feed updates to the selected category and sub-filter, and the empty state appears when nothing matches.
FEED-05Open a post viewer and use the post actions.Select a post tile, like it, add a comment, save it, share it, and close the viewer.The viewer opens, the actions respond, the comment appears, save state toggles, and closing returns to the feed.
FEED-06Save a feed post and confirm the saved state persists.Save a post from the feed, refresh the page, and open /saved to confirm the post is still stored.The save button stays active after refresh and the item appears in the Saved page until it is removed.

Stories, Reels, and Video Playback

Checks the media-heavy social surfaces, including privacy and player behavior.

Case Scenario Steps Expected Result
STORY-01Create and publish a story.Open /story/create; add media and privacy; publish the story.The story is created successfully and becomes visible in the story surface for eligible viewers.
STORY-02Private stories stay hidden from non-followers.View a private account as a non-follower; open /stories or the relevant profile story surface.The story does not appear to unauthorized users.
STORY-03Followers can view stories and view counts update.Open the story as an approved follower; watch it; reload the story feed.The follower can view the story and the view counter increments once per viewer as expected.
STORY-04Only one video plays at a time in reels or long-video feeds.Open /clips or /watch; start playback on one video, then start another.The previous video pauses so the feed does not play multiple clips simultaneously.
